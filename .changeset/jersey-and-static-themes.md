Two new themes.

- Jersey: 90s pro sports, expansion-team era. Teal, purple and black with a basketball orange; varsity block titles (Graduate) in a two-color tackle-twill outline; trading-card panels with a frame, a corner team stripe, a slanted nameplate title and a holo-foil sheen on the featured card; mesh keys in broadcast italics (Barlow Semi Condensed); stitched patch badges and avatars; Starter-jacket stripes on the navbar and sidebar; a dot-matrix scoreboard (Doto) for toasts, tooltips and progress. Hardwood by day, the arena after dark. Adds `board` and `bulb` tokens.
- Static: early-2000s alt rock. A sketchbook by day (aged paper, photocopy grain, pencil hatching, off-register red stencil titles in Big Shoulders Stencil) and a spray-painted concrete wall after dark. Duct-taped, slightly crooked pages; rough-cut stencil keys that glitch on hover; Dymo-tape badges and tooltips (Special Elite); pin-badge avatars; an amp toggle with a pilot light; a VU meter for progress; an equalizer spinner; toasts in a media-player LCD. Adds `tape` and `lcd-green` tokens.
