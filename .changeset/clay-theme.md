New Clay theme: plasticine and Play-Doh. Puffy, slightly lumpy slabs with a matte grain, a hand-pressed mottle and a thumbprint in each card; a featured card marbled from three colors; keys that are clay blobs with a thickness underneath, squashing flat when pressed and springing back; fields pressed into the clay; rolled-pill badges; clay-ball avatars, checkboxes and switch thumbs; a rolled snake under tabs and a twisted rainbow snake under the navbar; a twisted two-color clay rope for progress; four bouncing clay balls for the spinner; toasts that plop down as a slab of plum clay. Bagel Fat One lettering, extruded on a layer of yellow, over Nunito. A craft table by day, a green cutting mat after dark. Adds `clay-blue`, `clay-green`, `clay-pink` and `clay-purple` tokens. Import `themes/clay.css` and `themes/clay-fonts.css`.
