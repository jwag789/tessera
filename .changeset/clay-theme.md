A new theme, Clay: playdough and claymation. Every surface is a soft matte lump of modelling clay in Play-Doh colors (blue, red, yellow, green, purple) on warm putty, or dark plasticine after dark, with a lit clay grain, the odd thumbprint and a few clay balls left lying around the page. Corners are lumpy and hand-rolled; big titles are fat Fredoka molded from clay like the keys (a lit top edge, a shaded belly, grain pressed into the letters, a thick edge underneath); keys are colored lumps and interactive cards are soft slabs, both wobbling on hover, squashing when pressed and springing back; fields, checkboxes, switches and segmented controls are pressed into the clay; the featured card is a yellow slab on a red one; dialogs and toasts plop in; progress is a rolled two-tone clay snake, steps are beads, the spinner is four balls bouncing with squash and stretch. Body type is Figtree, mono is Space Mono. Adds a `clay-purple` token.

Import `themes/clay.css` and `themes/clay-fonts.css`.
