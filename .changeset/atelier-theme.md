New theme, Atelier: editorial and quiet, for studios and portfolios. Warm bone paper and near-black ink; titles in a light, high-contrast serif (Fraunces) over Manrope; keys, labels, bar links and badges in small spaced capitals; hairlines instead of shadows, square corners, fields as a single rule, a brochure-style accordion, and one terracotta accent. After dark it's a gallery wall in deep charcoal with ivory type. Import `@tessera-design/react/themes/atelier.css` and `themes/atelier-fonts.css`, then set `brand="atelier"`.
