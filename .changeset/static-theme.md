New Static theme: early-2000s alt rock. A sketchbook by day (aged paper, photocopy grain, pencil hatching, off-register red stencil titles in Big Shoulders Stencil) and a spray-painted concrete wall after dark. Duct-taped, slightly crooked pages; rough-cut stencil keys that glitch on hover; Dymo-tape badges and tooltips (Special Elite); pin-badge avatars; an amp toggle with a pilot light; a VU meter for progress; an equalizer spinner; toasts in a media-player LCD. Adds `tape` and `lcd-green` tokens. Import `themes/static.css` and `themes/static-fonts.css`.
