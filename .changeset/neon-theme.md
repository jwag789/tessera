New Neon theme: the 80s after dark. A synthwave sun sinks behind a glowing perspective grid; big titles are a red neon outline in Merriweather Black; keys, tabs and badges are Audiowide arcade lettering that lights up (a sunset primary, a cyan-outline secondary, an "insert coin" yellow accent that pulses); fields, tooltips and toasts are CRT terminals in VT323 with scanlines; panels are dark glass with a neon edge and VHS color fringing on their titles; the featured card is a neon sign that flickers on; a string of Christmas lights chases along the navbar and blinks in the spinner; progress is an arcade power meter; dialogs switch on like a CRT. Light mode is the same scene in pastel daylight. Adds `crt` and `crt-ink` tokens. Import `themes/neon.css` and `themes/neon-fonts.css`.
