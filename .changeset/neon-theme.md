New Neon theme: Tron. A black grid world lit in cyan, with orange for the other side. Big titles are Orbitron capitals, white-hot with a cyan glow and a slice through the middle; keys, tabs and badges are Orbitron capitals too, over Oxanium. Panels are dark glass with HUD brackets on their corners; the featured card is orange and flickers as it rezzes in. Light-cycle trails run along the navbar and fill the progress bar. Fields, tooltips and toasts are terminals in Share Tech Mono with phosphor-cyan type; dialogs boot from a line of light; avatars are identity discs; a grid floor runs to a glowing horizon behind the page. Light mode is the white grid. Adds `crt` and `crt-ink` tokens. Import `themes/neon.css` and `themes/neon-fonts.css`.
