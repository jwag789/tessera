A new theme, Lagoon: a PS2-era JRPG island. A sunlit beach by day (sand, a pale sky, the tide rolling in along the bottom of the screen) and a starlit shore after dark with pyreflies drifting up. Menus, dialogs, popovers, tooltips and toasts are the classic blue gradient window with a white edge in both modes, with a bobbing pointing-glove cursor; titles are Marcellus with a sea-glass sheen; keys are glossy gel and the accent key is a gold sphere; the featured card sits in a gold window frame; avatars are element orbs; progress is an overdrive gauge; the spinner is four orbs in orbit; toasts read like an item-get box; the navbar ends in a wave. Body type is Nunito, mono is DM Mono.

Import `themes/lagoon.css` and `themes/lagoon-fonts.css`.
