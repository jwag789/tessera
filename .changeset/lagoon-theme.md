A new theme, Lagoon: a PS2-era JRPG island. A sunlit beach by day (a big sky with the sun and a few clouds, sand along the foot of the page) and the night tide after dark (the moon, a field of stars, the sea glowing at the foot, pyreflies drifting up). Menus, dialogs, popovers, tooltips and toasts are the classic blue gradient window with a white edge in both modes, with a bobbing pointing-glove cursor; titles are Marcellus with a sea-glass sheen; keys are glossy gel and the accent key is a gold sphere; the featured card sits in a gold window frame; avatars are element orbs; progress is an overdrive gauge; the spinner is four orbs in orbit; toasts read like an item-get box; the navbar ends in a wave. Body type is Nunito, mono is DM Mono.

Import `themes/lagoon.css` and `themes/lagoon-fonts.css`.
