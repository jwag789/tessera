New theme: Lagoon. A PS2-era JRPG island, after Final Fantasy X and Chrono Cross. By day a sunlit beach (a big sky, the sun and clouds, sand along the foot of the page); after dark the night tide under the moon, with stars and pyreflies drifting up. Menus, dialogs, popovers, the command palette, tooltips and toasts are classic blue menu windows with a silver edge and drop-shadowed white text, and a white glove points at the active item. Cinzel titles over M PLUS Rounded 1c; sea-glass keys that glint; cards with a lagoon swell along the foot and a sunset rim on the featured one; a wave for the tab underline; elemental-orb avatars and spinner; an ATB gauge for progress; a pearl on the switch. Adds `window`, `window-deep` and `pyrefly` tokens.

Import `themes/lagoon.css` and `themes/lagoon-fonts.css`.
