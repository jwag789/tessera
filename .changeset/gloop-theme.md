New Gloop theme, replacing Soft (which never shipped in a release): 90s kids-TV energy with orange primary keys, slime green, grape and teal, thick black outlines, offset shadows in a clashing color, blob-shaped badges and tile keys, a slime drip under the navbar, a squiggle under the selected tab, cards tilted a little either way, and Luckiest Guy lettering over Fredoka. Keys squash when pressed, cards wobble and toasts bounce in, all off under reduced motion. Import `@tessera/react/themes/gloop.css` and `@tessera/react/themes/gloop-fonts.css`, then set `brand="gloop"`. Theme builds now clear old output, so removed themes don't linger in `dist/themes`.
