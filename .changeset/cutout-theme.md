New theme, Cutout: construction paper and scissors, like a cutout cartoon. A sky of blue paper with paper clouds over a row of snowy paper hills, or a navy night with a paper moon and stars after dark, with construction-paper grain laid over the whole page. Everything is cut from paper in flat colors: scissor-cut corners, sheets laid down a little crooked with a hard little shadow, chunky Lilita One titles cut out with a white margin, Mali for body text and Courier Prime for mono. Keys and cards don't glide; they jump a frame at a time, the way cutout animation moves, and pressing pastes them flat. The featured card is yellow paper, badges and tooltips are snipped scraps, avatars are paper circles, the navbar ends in a scissor-cut edge, progress is a red strip glued on in jumps and the spinner is four scraps hopping in turn. Import `@tessera-design/react/themes/cutout.css` and `themes/cutout-fonts.css`, then set `brand="cutout"`.
