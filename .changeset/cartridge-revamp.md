**Cartridge** goes further than a palette. Every card is a cartridge with the label set into a plastic shell, and interactive ones tip toward you on hover. On its own, the mosaic tips back in perspective into a stage floor of raised blocks, and the page sits on a checkerboard floor that fades into distance fog. Primary, secondary and tile keys carry a round A, B or C prompt; danger is the Start key and rumbles when pressed; focus is a breathing yellow lock-on. Dialogs, popovers, toasts and tooltips are see-through text boxes (toasts wait on a blinking red arrow, dialogs zoom in out of the fog). Progress is a magic meter, the slider thumb is an analog stick, the switch is a power slider, accent badges are coins and avatars are shaded spheres. New `key-a`, `key-b`, `key-c` and `key-start` tokens (with `on-*` pairs) hold the controller colors. Display type is Titan One, so reload `themes/cartridge-fonts.css`.
