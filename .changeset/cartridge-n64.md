Cartridge redone as a Nintendo 64 theme instead of a recolor. Primary is the blue A button (domed, white label, presses down), danger is the red Start button, the accent is the yellow C button. Cards are cartridges: the label sits in grey plastic with grip ridges across the top, and the featured card is the gold one. Dialogs, popovers, menus, selects, tooltips and toasts are Zelda-style text boxes (dark navy, yellow titles, a blinking green arrow on dialogs, an A-blue menu cursor). Display headings get Mario 64 gold, extruded lettering. Progress is the magic meter, switches are the power slider with a red light, avatars are face buttons and mosaic tiles are shaded cubes. Focus rings are yellow.
