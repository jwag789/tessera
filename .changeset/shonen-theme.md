New Shonen theme: 90s anime. Light mode is Saturday morning, with a pale anime-sky ground and a fade of manga screentone, a blue primary, an electric-yellow tile key and a red navbar. Dark mode is late at night, with Unit-01 purple, an acid-green primary, warning orange, a faint hex grid, hazard stripes under the navbar and headings in a heavy mincho serif. Both modes have cel-shaded keys (a hard two-tone shade, black line art and a glint), a tile key that powers up with a flickering aura on hover, speed lines behind dialogs, slanted badges, tabs and current-page highlights, toasts styled as a battle text box with a double frame and a blinking arrow, and progress shown as an HP bar. Dela Gothic One headings over M PLUS Rounded 1c, with Shippori Mincho B1 after dark. Motion is off under reduced motion. Import `@tessera-design/react/themes/shonen.css` and `@tessera-design/react/themes/shonen-fonts.css`, then set `brand="shonen"`.
