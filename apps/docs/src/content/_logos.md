# Logos

- `tessera-mark.svg` — The mark: three lapis tiles and one saffron tile knocked out of the grid. Use on `bg` or `surface` in the light theme. Minimum size 20px; clear space of one tile (18/48 of its width) on every side.
- `tessera-mark-mono.svg` — Single ink, filled `ink` (#0b0e18). Use on saffron, on photography, and anywhere color printing isn't available. On dark grounds, recolor the fill to `#eef0f7`.

Set the word "Tessera" beside the mark in `heading-md` (Bricolage Grotesque 700), `space-2` gap. There is no separate wordmark file.
