# Checkbox

An independent on/off choice that takes effect when the form is submitted. For an instant setting, use Switch.

**Consumer provides:** `label`, native `checked`/`defaultChecked`/`onChange`/`disabled`/`name`.

- The whole row, label included, is the hit target.
- Labels state the "on" condition positively: "Show my phone number", not "Don't hide my phone number".
