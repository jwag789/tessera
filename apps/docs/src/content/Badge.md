# Badge

A short, non-interactive status or category label.

**Consumer provides:** `children` (one or two words), optional `tone` (`neutral` · `brand` · `success` · `warning` · `danger` · `accent`), `dot`, and `live` (a dot that pulses).

- Status badges always carry a word; color is never the only signal.
- `live` is for things happening right now (a public link, a sync). One pulsing badge per view.
- `accent` (saffron with a 2px ink offset) is for "New" or "Featured" only, at most one per card.
- Badges are not buttons.
